CrunchE firmware updater

No software to install. This page talks to your CrunchE directly over USB.

  1. Plug CrunchE into the computer with a data USB‑C cable.
  2. Press Connect below and choose the USB Serial / 303A:1001 port.
  3. Press Install. It reboots itself when finished.

If no port appears: hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT, then press Connect.

Your browser can’t open serial ports. Use Chrome, Edge, or Firefox 151+ on a computer — Safari (including iPhone) has no Web Serial support. This updater must be opened over HTTPS.
